How readings are captured

Staff submit meter readings from the field app with a photograph of the dial attached. Entries without a photo are marked estimated and excluded from variance flags. Readings queue locally when signal drops in the Cajuiles and Barranca sectors and sync on reconnect, so the monthly figure is closed from captured data rather than reconstructed at month end.
